## Parking & Driving Tips for Gurgaon

- **Golf Course Road Congestion:** Avoid this stretch between 6-9 PM. Use the Southern Peripheral Road as an alternative.
- **Delhi-Gurgaon Expressway Toll:** FASTag is mandatory. All Brothers vehicles come with an active FASTag pre-installed.
- **Mall Parking:** Ambience Mall and DLF Mega Mall offer ample covered parking — arrive before noon on weekends.
- **Speed Cameras:** NH48 has active speed cameras. Maintain 100 km/h to avoid e-challans on your rental.
