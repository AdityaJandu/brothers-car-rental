Renting a self-drive car in India has never been easier — but understanding the local rules can save you significant time, money, and stress. Whether you're a first-time domestic renter or an international tourist, this guide covers everything from vehicle booking to final drop-off.

## Who is Eligible to Rent a Car in India?

To legally rent and drive a car without a chauffeur in India, you must meet these criteria:

- Be at least **21 years of age** (premium and luxury car rentals may require drivers to be 25+).
- Hold a valid Indian driving licence or an International Driving Permit (IDP) for tourists.
- Provide a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar or Passport).

## Required Documents for Car Hire

Keep these original documents ready when you pick up your vehicle:

1. **Driving Licence** — Original physical copy (DigiLocker is accepted at Brothers).
2. **Photo ID Proof** — Valid passport or voter ID.
3. **Address Proof** — Utility bill or bank statement (only if your current address differs from your ID).

## Understanding Car Rental Pricing in India

When comparing car rental prices across platforms, look out for these core components:

- **Daily Base Rate** — Fluctuates based on vehicle category (hatchback vs SUV) and seasonal holiday demand.
- **Damage Protection Fee** — Covers collision damage waivers and 24/7 roadside assistance.
- **Surcharges** — Applicable for one-way outstation drops, late vehicle returns, or airport pickup convenience fees.
