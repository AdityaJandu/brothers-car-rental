## Driving Tips for Ghaziabad

- **NH24 Traffic:** This is one of NCR's busiest corridors. Use the Delhi-Meerut Expressway as an alternative to bypass the Ghaziabad core.
- **Indirapuram Parking:** Sector malls (Shipra Mall, Mahagun Mall) offer free covered parking. Avoid street parking on the main Ahinsa Khand road.
- **UP Border Checkpoints:** Occasionally, UP police conduct random document checks at the Delhi-Ghaziabad border. Keep your licence and rental agreement handy.
