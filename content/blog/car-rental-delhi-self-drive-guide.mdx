## Navigating Delhi Traffic: Essential Tips for Self-Drive Renters

Driving in Delhi is an acquired skill, but with the right mindset, it becomes second nature within hours:


- **Use Google Maps Live Traffic:** Always check real-time traffic before departing. The difference between a 20-minute and a 90-minute commute often comes down to choosing the right route at the right time.
- **Avoid Peak Hours:** 8:30-10:30 AM and 5:30-8:00 PM are gridlock windows. Plan your major drives outside these brackets.
- **Master the Roundabouts:** Delhi is famous for its multi-lane roundabouts (India Gate, ITO, Ashram). Yield to traffic already in the circle, signal early, and don't panic.
- **Toll-Free Inner Routes:** The Ring Road and Outer Ring Road are toll-free arterial highways that connect most parts of Delhi efficiently.
- **Parking Strategy:** Use DDA multi-level parking complexes in CP, Sarojini Nagar, and Lajpat Nagar. Street parking invites challans.
