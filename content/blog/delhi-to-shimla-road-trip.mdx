## Route Breakdown

### Delhi → Chandigarh (260 km, ~4.5 hours)

The NH44 from Delhi to Chandigarh is one of India's best highways — a wide, well-maintained expressway through Haryana. Cruise at 100 km/h, pass through Karnal and Ambala, and reach Chandigarh refreshed. This is premium [sedan territory](/browse?category=sedan "Sedan rental Delhi to Shimla").


### Chandigarh → Shimla (90 km, ~2.5 hours)

The real driving begins. NH5 climbs from Chandigarh through Pinjore Gardens, the charming town of Solan (famous for its brewery and mushroom farming), and onward through pine forests to Shimla. The mountain section features moderate gradients with well-maintained roads.


## Best Car for Delhi to Shimla

- **Ideal: Compact SUV.** The Delhi-Chandigarh highway demands comfort; the Chandigarh-Shimla ascent demands torque and clearance. An [SUV](/browse?category=suv "SUV rental Delhi to Shimla") excels at both.
- **Good Alternative: Premium Sedan.** The Shimla road is well-paved enough for sedans. The mountain gradients are gentler than Mussoorie or Manali routes.


## Must-Visit Pit Stops

- **Haveli, Karnal:** A famous highway restaurant on NH44. Excellent food and clean facilities.
- **Sukhna Lake, Chandigarh:** If time permits, a 30-minute stop at this serene lake refreshes you before the mountain drive.
- **Pinjore Gardens:** Mughal-era terraced gardens just outside Chandigarh. A peaceful 20-minute stroll.
- **Solan Brewery Tour:** India's oldest brewery. If visiting during weekdays, a quick tour is possible (check timings).


## Shimla Driving & Parking Tips

- **Mall Road is Pedestrian-Only:** You cannot drive on Shimla's famous Mall Road. Park at the circular road below and walk up, or use the lift to the Mall.
- **Parking Lots:** Multi-level parking at Sanjauli, Victory Tunnel, and the ISBT area. Rates are ₹50-100/day.
- **One-Way System:** Shimla has a complex one-way traffic system. Follow Google Maps religiously.
- **Winter Snow:** December-February can bring snow to Shimla's roads. SUV with good tyres is essential. Chains may be required for Kufri and beyond.


## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I drive from Delhi to Shimla in one day?

Yes. Starting at 5-6 AM from Delhi, you'll reach Shimla by 1-2 PM. The drive is comfortable for experienced drivers. For a more relaxed experience, break the journey in Chandigarh for lunch.


### Is the Chandigarh-Shimla road safe for beginners?

The Chandigarh-Shimla road (NH5) is one of the better-maintained mountain roads in India. Gradients are moderate, curves are well-banked, and road markings are clear. It's an excellent introductory mountain drive for confident city drivers.


### What are the toll charges from Delhi to Shimla?

Total toll charges are approximately ₹500-600 one way (Delhi to Chandigarh section). The Chandigarh-Shimla road has no toll plazas. All Brothers rentals include active FASTag.


### Can I extend from Shimla to Kufri or Narkanda?

Absolutely. Kufri (16 km from Shimla) and Narkanda (65 km) are stunning extensions. Kufri is accessible to all vehicles, but Narkanda's road can be icy in winter — SUV recommended.


### Is parking available in Shimla during peak tourist season?

Shimla parking is notoriously tight during May-June and December-January. Arrive before 10 AM for the best chance at finding a spot. Hotels with private parking are your safest bet.
