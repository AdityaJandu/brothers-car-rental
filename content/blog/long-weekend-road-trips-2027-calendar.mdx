## How to Maximize Long Weekends

- **Take 1 day of leave, get 4 days off:** When a holiday falls on Tuesday or Thursday, taking one strategic leave day creates a 4-day weekend. Plan your entire year's leave around these opportunities.
- **Friday evening departures:** Leave after work on Friday to get an extra half-day at the destination. Return Sunday evening for Monday-off holidays.
- **Book rental cars 2-3 weeks before:** Long weekend demand spikes rental prices by 15-25% as the date approaches. Early booking locks in regular rates.
- **Book accommodation 3-4 weeks before:** Popular destinations sell out fast. Confirmed bookings also let you plan the drive schedule precisely.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which long weekend is best for a first road trip?

Republic Day weekend (4 days in January). The weather is cool, highways are relatively empty (most people celebrate at home), and destinations like Jaipur and Agra are at their most comfortable.


### How much should I budget per long weekend trip?

For a 3-day trip with 4 friends splitting costs: ₹2,500-4,000 per person (rental + fuel + tolls + accommodation + food). Self-drive road trips are among the most affordable travel options in India.


### Should I return on the last day or the night before?

Return on the last day (morning departure from the destination) to maximize vacation time. Start by 8 AM to reach Delhi before the evening return-traffic crush.


### What if the long weekend destination is fully booked?

Popular destinations book out fast. Alternatives: instead of Mussoorie, try Dhanaulti or Kanatal. Instead of Shimla, try Kasauli. Instead of Nainital, try Mukteshwar. The less-known destinations are often more rewarding.
