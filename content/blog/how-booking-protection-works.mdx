When you rent a self-drive vehicle, peace of mind matters just as much as the car itself. Our damage protection plans are specifically designed to keep you covered for the unexpected on Indian roads — without any confusing fine print or hidden clauses.


## What's Included in Your Base Car Hire Plan?

Every single Brothers vehicle booking comes with **Standard Protection** integrated at no extra cost:


- **Third-Party Liability:** Coverage as strictly required by Indian motor vehicle laws.
- **24/7 Roadside Assistance (RSA):** Immediate help for flat tyres, dead batteries, towing, or key lockouts.
- **Accidental Damage Waivers:** Caps your financial liability to a small, fixed excess threshold in the event of an accident.


## Comprehensive Protection Upgrade (Zero-Excess Rental)

For complete highway reassurance, upgrading to our Comprehensive Protection plan is the smartest move. It adds:


- **Zero-Excess Liability:** You pay absolutely nothing out-of-pocket for accidental exterior scratches, dents, or collisions.
- **Tyre & Glass Coverage:** Pothole blowouts and windshield pebble chips are fully covered.
- **Interior Protection:** Waivers for accidental upholstery stains or spill damages.
- **Theft Security:** Full financial protection if the vehicle is stolen.


## What is NOT Covered?

To be perfectly transparent, no rental protection plan covers damage caused by driving under the influence of alcohol, driving on prohibited off-road terrain (like flooded rivers or beaches), or traffic/speeding fines incurred during your rental period.
