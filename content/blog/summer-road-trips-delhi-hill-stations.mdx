## 1. Kasauli — The Quick Escape (290 km, 5.5 hrs)

The closest hill station via Chandigarh. Kasauli's charm is in its smallness — a quiet cantonment town of colonial bungalows and pine forests. The drive via NH44 to Chandigarh is expressway-easy, followed by a gentle 30 km mountain ascent. Best car: [sedan](/browse?category=sedan "Sedan for Kasauli") (mountain gradients are mild).


## 2. Mussoorie — The Classic (290 km, 6.5 hrs)

The Queen of Hills never disappoints. Misty mornings, Mall Road evenings, and Kempty Falls picnics define the Mussoorie summer. Best car: [compact SUV](/browse?category=suv "SUV for Mussoorie") for the 35 km mountain ascent. [Full drive guide](/blog/delhi-to-mussoorie-road-trip-car-rental "Delhi to Mussoorie road trip").


## 3. Shimla — The Grand (350 km, 7 hrs)

The erstwhile British summer capital offers the most developed hill station infrastructure. Colonial architecture, the iconic Mall Road, and Christ Church create a distinctly Victorian mountain town experience. Best car: SUV or premium sedan. [Full drive guide](/blog/delhi-to-shimla-road-trip "Delhi to Shimla guide").


## 4. Lansdowne — The Secret (260 km, 6 hrs)

The car-free cantonment that tourists haven't discovered. Oak forests, military heritage, and silence. Best car: [SUV](/browse?category=suv "SUV for Lansdowne") for the last 40 km. [Full drive guide](/blog/delhi-to-lansdowne-road-trip "Delhi to Lansdowne guide").


## 5. Nainital — The Lake Town (300 km, 7 hrs)

Naini Lake, boating, the colonial-era Mall Road, and the panoramic viewpoints of Snow View and Tiffin Top. Best car: compact SUV for the Haldwani-Nainital ascent.


## 6. Dhanaulti — The Offbeat (310 km, 7 hrs)

Beyond Mussoorie, Dhanaulti sits at 2,250 metres in pristine rhododendron and deodar forests. Far quieter and cooler than Mussoorie. Best car: SUV (road beyond Mussoorie can be rough).


## 7. Kanatal — The Hideaway (300 km, 7.5 hrs)

A tiny hamlet beyond Dhanaulti at 2,500 metres. Apple orchards, camping sites, and the Surkanda Devi trek. Best car: SUV mandatory for the last 20 km.


## 8. Chakrata — The Adventure (330 km, 7 hrs)

A cantonment town with Tiger Falls, trekking, and rock climbing. Less accessible = less crowded. Best car: mid-size SUV with good ground clearance.


## Summer Hill Station Tips

- **Book 3-4 weeks in advance:** May-June is absolute peak season. Hotels and rental cars sell out fast.
- **Start at 4-5 AM:** Beat Delhi's morning traffic and arrive at the hill station by noon before tourist traffic peaks.
- **Carry light woolens:** Hill station evenings can drop to 15-18°C even in summer. A light jacket is essential.
- **AC strategy:** Use AC on the highway; switch to open windows during the mountain ascent for natural cool mountain air.
