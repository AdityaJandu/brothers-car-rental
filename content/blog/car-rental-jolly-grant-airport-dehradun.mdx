## Airport Rental Tips for Jolly Grant

- **Book in Advance:** Jolly Grant is a small airport with limited flight slots. Rental demand concentrates during the morning arrival window (8 AM - 12 PM). Pre-booking guarantees your specific vehicle category.
- **Fuel Strategy:** There's a petrol station on the airport road (Doiwala). Fill up before heading to Mussoorie or the hills, where fuel stations are scarce.
- **Monsoon Awareness:** During July-August, check road status toward Mussoorie and Rishikesh before departing. Occasional landslides can temporarily close mountain routes.
- **Return Planning:** For your return flight, arrive at the airport car drop zone at least 2 hours before departure. Our team will inspect and close your booking within 15 minutes.
