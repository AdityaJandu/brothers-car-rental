Nothing ruins the excitement of a road trip faster than showing up to the rental counter without the right paperwork. Here is the definitive, up-to-date checklist of **documents required for self-drive car rentals in India**.


## Document Checklist for Indian Residents

1. **Valid Driving Licence:** Must be the original document (not a photocopy) and strictly not expired. Both physical smart cards and DigiLocker verified licences are widely accepted.
2. **Government Photo ID:** A valid Aadhaar card, passport, or voter ID card.
3. **Address Proof:** If your photo ID doesn't display your current residential address, bring a recent utility bill or bank statement.


## Requirements for International Tourists & NRIs

1. **International Driving Permit (IDP):** Issued by your home country. This must be carried alongside your original foreign driving licence.
2. **Valid Passport & Indian Visa:** Original copies required for security verification.
3. **Local Address Proof:** Your hotel booking confirmation or a formal letter from your local host.

- **Paperless Uploads:** At Brothers Car Rental, you can complete your KYC entirely by uploading documents during the online checkout. Enjoy zero paperwork at the pickup location.
