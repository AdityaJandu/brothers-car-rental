Featuring expansive multi-lane highways like the NH9 and NH44 corridors, Haryana offers some of the smoothest, most relaxed driving experiences in North India. It is a fantastic landscape for long, effortless highway cruises without the stress of mountain switchbacks.


## Top Historical & Cultural Driving Stops

- **Kurukshetra:** The epicentre of the Mahabharata. Visit the massive Brahma Sarovar.
- **Agroha Dham (Hisar):** A massive, beautiful temple complex perfect for a peaceful afternoon stop.
- **Morni Hills:** The only hill station in Haryana, offering a gentle, highly scenic mountain drive near Panchkula.
