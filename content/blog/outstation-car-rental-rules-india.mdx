Renting a car for an outstation trip is different from a quick city run. Interstate travel involves permits, toll management, mileage considerations, and specific return protocols. Understanding these **outstation car rental rules** before you book saves you from unexpected charges and ensures a smooth cross-state road trip.


## Interstate Permit: Can I Drive Across State Borders?

Yes. All Brothers Car Rental vehicles carry **all-India tourist vehicle permits** that allow unrestricted travel across all Indian states and union territories. You do not need to arrange any additional permits or pay border-crossing fees. Drive from Delhi to Rajasthan, Uttarakhand, Punjab, or anywhere else without paperwork.


## Mileage Policies for Outstation Trips

Understanding the mileage structure prevents bill-shock at return:


- **Unlimited Kilometres:** Brothers Car Rental offers unlimited km packages for most outstation bookings. You drive as far as you need without watching the odometer.
- **Fair Use Policy:** Some extremely long-duration rentals (30+ days) may have reasonable mileage caps clearly stated at booking. Always check the booking summary for specifics.
- **Per-Km Packages:** Available for budget-conscious renters who know their exact route distance. Lower daily rate with a per-km charge beyond the included allowance.


## One-Way vs Round-Trip Outstation Rentals

### Round-Trip (Pick up and return at the same location)

The most common and cost-effective option. Rent the car from your home city, drive to the destination, and return the car at the same pickup location. No additional one-way fees apply.


### One-Way (Pick up in City A, drop in City B)

Available for select routes (Delhi↔Jaipur, Delhi↔Chandigarh, Delhi↔Dehradun, etc.). A one-way convenience fee (typically ₹1,000-3,000 depending on the route) applies to cover the cost of repositioning the vehicle. [Check one-way availability](/browse "One-way car rental options").


## Outstation Driving Documents

For interstate travel, always carry:


- **Original Driving Licence:** Highway police and state border checkpoints frequently verify licences.
- **Rental Agreement:** A digital or printed copy of your Brothers rental agreement. This proves the vehicle is legally rented and not stolen.
- **Photo ID:** Aadhaar card or passport for identification.
- **Vehicle RC Copy:** Kept in the car's document holder. All Brothers vehicles have valid RC and insurance papers in the glove box.
- **Insurance Certificate:** Also kept in the vehicle. Verify it's current before departing.


## What Happens at State Borders?

Most state borders no longer have physical checkpoints for passenger cars. However, you may encounter:


- **Random police checks:** Usually in UP, Rajasthan, and Haryana. Keep documents handy.
- **Toll plazas:** FASTag (included in all Brothers vehicles) handles toll payments automatically.
- **Agricultural checkpoints:** Occasionally in states like Maharashtra. These apply to commercial vehicles only — passenger cars pass through freely.


## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I take a rental car to restricted areas like Ladakh or Spiti?

You can drive to Ladakh and Spiti, but some zones (like the Pangong Lake area and Nubra Valley) require Inner Line Permits (ILP) obtained from the District Magistrate's office in Leh. The vehicle permit covers the car; you need the ILP for personal access.


### Is there a daily minimum km charge for outstation trips?

Brothers Car Rental doesn't impose a minimum daily kilometre charge. You pay for the rental days, and kilometre packages (limited or unlimited) are selected at booking.


### What if the car breaks down on an outstation trip?

Call our 24/7 roadside assistance hotline. Depending on the issue, we'll either dispatch a mechanic, arrange a tow, or replace the vehicle entirely at the nearest city. All breakdown support is included in your rental at no extra cost.


### Can I extend an outstation rental while on the trip?

Yes. Call our support team to extend by any number of days. The extension is charged at the same daily rate as your original booking. No penalties or surge pricing.


### Is there a late return penalty for outstation trips?

A grace period of 30-60 minutes is standard. Beyond that, late returns are charged on a per-hour basis (typically ₹150-300/hour depending on vehicle category). If you foresee a delay, call ahead to officially extend and avoid penalties.
