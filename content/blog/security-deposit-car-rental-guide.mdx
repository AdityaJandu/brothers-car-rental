## Why Do Rental Companies Charge a Deposit?

The security deposit serves as a financial safeguard against:


- **Accidental damage:** Scratches, dents, or collision damage beyond normal wear and tear.
- **Traffic violations:** E-challans for speeding, red-light jumping, or parking violations incurred during your rental period.
- **Fuel shortfall:** If you return the car with less fuel than at pickup under a Full-to-Full policy.
- **Late returns:** Penalties for returning the vehicle past the agreed drop-off time.
- **Toll charges:** Any FASTag toll charges accumulated during your trip.


## How and When is the Deposit Refunded?

At Brothers Car Rental, the refund process is straightforward:


1. **Vehicle Return:** Return the car at the agreed time and location.
2. **Inspection (10-15 min):** Our team does a quick walkaround inspection for damage, fuel level, and cleanliness.
3. **Refund Initiation:** If everything checks out, the refund is initiated immediately.
4. **Refund Timeline:** UPI refunds: 24-48 hours. Debit Card: 5-7 business days. Credit Card: 7-10 business days (depends on your bank).


## Deposit Payment Methods

- **UPI (Fastest Refund):** Pay via Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm. Refunds are typically processed within 24-48 hours. [Learn more about UPI payments](/blog/can-you-rent-a-car-without-credit-card "Rent without credit card").
- **Debit Card:** Visa, Mastercard, and RuPay accepted. Refund timeline: 5-7 business days.
- **Credit Card:** Most widely accepted. The deposit may appear as a pre-authorization hold that's released upon return, rather than a charge-and-refund cycle.


## What Can Be Deducted from the Deposit?

- **Damage beyond wear and tear:** Significant scratches, dents, cracked glass, or interior damage. Minor surface scratches from normal use are not charged.
- **Traffic challans:** Any e-challans issued to the vehicle during your rental period.
- **Fuel difference:** If returned with less fuel than at pickup.
- **Excessive cleaning:** If the car is returned in an excessively dirty condition (e.g., mud-caked interior, food stains).


## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I rent a car without paying a security deposit?

Standard rentals require a security deposit. However, our monthly subscription plans and certain promotional offers may include reduced or waived deposits for qualifying customers.


### What happens if there's damage to the rental car?

If damage is found during the return inspection, the repair cost is deducted from your deposit. If the damage exceeds the deposit amount, you'll be billed for the difference. Our Comprehensive Protection plan eliminates this risk entirely — [learn about our protection plans](/blog/how-booking-protection-works "Booking protection explained").


### Is the deposit the same as the rental payment?

No. The deposit is separate from your rental booking payment. You pay both the rental fee and the deposit at checkout. The rental fee is non-refundable; the deposit is fully refundable upon clean vehicle return.


### Can I pay the deposit in cash?

Brothers Car Rental operates as a digital-first platform. Deposits must be paid via digital methods (UPI, debit card, or credit card). Cash deposits are not accepted as they complicate the refund process.


### How do I dispute a deposit deduction?

If you disagree with a deduction, contact our support team within 48 hours of the deduction notification. We'll review the inspection photos, walkaround video, and your pre-rental documentation to resolve any disputes fairly.
