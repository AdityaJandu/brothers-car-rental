A common hurdle for many travellers is the industry-wide requirement for premium credit cards to secure a vehicle. At Brothers Car Rental, we believe in radical accessibility. You do **not** need a credit card to rent a self-drive car from us.


## Flexible Digital Payment Methods We Support

We have integrated modern Indian fintech solutions to make your booking process entirely seamless:


- **Debit Cards:** Visa, Mastercard, and RuPay debit cards are fully accepted for both your rental payments and security deposits.
- **UPI Payments:** Seamlessly pay via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM instantly directly from your bank account.
- **Digital Wallets:** Major Indian digital wallets are supported at our checkout gateway.


## How Do Security Deposits Work Without a Credit Card?

Instead of placing a pre-authorization hold on a credit card, you simply transfer the fully refundable security deposit via UPI or Debit Card during checkout. Once you return the vehicle in good condition, the refund is automatically initiated back to your original payment source within 5-7 business days.
