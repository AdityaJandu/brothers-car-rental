## How to Get the Cheapest Rental Rate

### 1. Book 2-3 Weeks in Advance

Rental algorithms increase prices as fleet availability drops. Booking early — especially for peak weekends and holiday periods — consistently yields rates 15-25% lower than last-minute bookings.


### 2. Choose the Right Category

Don't pay for an SUV when a hatchback will do. If you're driving within a city or on flat highways with 1-2 passengers, a [hatchback](/browse?category=hatchback "Budget hatchback rental") delivers the best per-day value with outstanding fuel economy (18-22 km/l).


### 3. Go Manual Transmission

Manual cars are 15-25% cheaper per day than automatic equivalents. If you're comfortable with a clutch and gearstick, manual is the instant budget choice.


### 4. Leverage Weekly and Monthly Discounts

Weekly rates offer 15-20% per-day savings over daily rates. Monthly subscriptions can drop the effective daily rate below ₹600/day for hatchbacks. If your trip is 5+ days, always check weekly pricing — extending to 7 days might actually cost less than a 5-day daily booking.


### 5. Skip Airport Pickup

Airport terminal pickups carry a convenience premium (₹200-500). If you can take a quick metro ride or cab to a city pickup point, you'll save the airport surcharge on top of a potentially lower daily rate at city locations.


### 6. Full-to-Full Fuel

Always return the car with a full tank. If the rental company refuels for you, they charge 1.5-2x market petrol prices. Filling up yourself at any pump saves ₹500-1,000 per trip.
