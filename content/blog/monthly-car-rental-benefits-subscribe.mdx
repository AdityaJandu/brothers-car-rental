Car ownership in India is getting harder to justify financially. A ₹12 lakh car costs ₹18+ lakh over 5 years when you add EMIs, insurance, maintenance, parking, and depreciation. Meanwhile, a **monthly car subscription** gives you the same mobility — a car in your driveway every morning — at a flat monthly fee with zero long-term commitment. Here's why India's urban professionals are switching to car subscriptions.


## How Monthly Car Subscription Works

1. **Choose your car:** Pick from hatchbacks, sedans, or SUVs based on your needs.
2. **Select your duration:** 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or 12 months. Longer = cheaper per month.
3. **Pay a flat monthly fee:** Covers the car, insurance, maintenance, and roadside assistance.
4. **Drive:** The car is delivered to your doorstep. Use it like your own car.
5. **Return or renew:** At the end of the subscription, return the car or renew for another term. Want a different car? Swap to a new one.

## Who Benefits Most from Monthly Subscriptions?

- **Professionals on transfers:** Posted to a new city for 6 months? Subscribe, drive, and return when you transfer out. No RTO transfer nightmares.
- **Startups and freelancers:** Your income fluctuates — a monthly car commitment matches your financial flexibility better than a 5-year EMI lock-in.
- **Testing before buying:** Want to experience a particular car model before committing ₹15+ lakh? Subscribe for a month and make an informed decision.
- **Second car needs:** Your family needs a second car for 3 months while your spouse commutes to a new office. Subscribe instead of buying.
- **NRIs on extended visits:** Spending 2-3 months in India? A monthly subscription is infinitely more convenient than daily cabs. [Read our NRI guide](/blog/nri-guide-renting-cars-india-vacation "NRI car rental guide").

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use a subscription car for outstation trips?

Yes. Monthly subscription cars carry all-India permits. Drive wherever you want — city, highway, mountain, or desert. No mileage restrictions on most plans.


### What if the car needs repairs during my subscription?

All maintenance and repair costs are ours. If the car has a mechanical issue, we repair it or replace it with another vehicle at no cost to you.


### Can I cancel a monthly subscription early?

Yes. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled with 7 days notice. No early termination fees (unlike car leases which penalize early returns).


### Is there a minimum subscription period?

1 month is the minimum. However, 3-month and 6-month subscriptions offer 10-20% better per-month rates.


### Can I swap to a different car during my subscription?

Yes. Want to upgrade from a sedan to an SUV for a family holiday? Request a swap — subject to availability and any rate difference. [View subscription options](/browse "View monthly subscription cars").
