## Critical Features for Family Cars

### 1. Rear AC Vents

Non-negotiable for Indian summer driving. Without rear AC vents, backseat passengers (especially children and elderly) suffer in 40°C+ heat. All our SUVs come with dual-zone AC.


### 2. Boot Space

Family road trips = more luggage than you planned. A minimum of 400 litres is essential for a 4-person family. SUVs with foldable third rows offer 500-700 litres.


### 3. Ride Quality

Children and elderly family members are more susceptible to motion sickness. Cars with good suspension that absorb bumps smoothly make a massive difference on Indian roads.


### 4. Safety Features

All Brothers rental cars include ABS, airbags, and seatbelts for every position. For families with infants, bring your own child car seat — some models have ISOFIX anchoring points.


### 5. USB Charging Ports

Multiple USB ports keep devices charged for navigation, entertainment, and the inevitable "are we there yet?" tablet sessions for kids.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I bring a child car seat in a rental?

Yes. Bring your own car seat — most modern rental cars have ISOFIX mounting points. Car seats are essential for children under 4 years on highways. Unfortunately, most Indian rental companies (including us) don't supply car seats as a rental add-on yet.


### Is an automatic car better for family trips?

Absolutely. Automatic transmission lets the driver focus entirely on the road and passengers, without clutch fatigue in traffic. With a car full of family, reduced driver distraction is a genuine safety benefit.


### What if a family member gets sick during the trip?

A self-drive car is actually better than public transport in this scenario — you can stop immediately, reach the nearest hospital or pharmacy without waiting for a bus to stop, and keep the patient comfortable in a private, air-conditioned space.


### How do I keep kids entertained on long drives?

Pre-download movies and shows on tablets (Netflix and Amazon Prime allow offline downloads). Pack colouring books and crayons. Play road trip games (spot the truck, number plate bingo). Schedule a pit stop every 90 minutes at interesting locations.
