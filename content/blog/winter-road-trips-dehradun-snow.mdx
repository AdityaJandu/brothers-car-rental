Winter in Uttarakhand is magical — pristine snow blankets the higher peaks, the air is crystal clear, and the Himalayan panoramas from Dehradun are at their most dramatic. For snow chasers and winter landscape photographers, Dehradun is the ideal base for accessing some of North India's most stunning winter destinations. Here's your guide to **winter road trips from Dehradun** with essential snow driving safety tips.


## Top Winter Destinations from Dehradun

### 1. Dhanaulti (60 km, ~2.5 hours)

The closest snow destination from Dehradun. Dhanaulti receives light snowfall in January-February, transforming its pine forests into a winter wonderland. The road via Mussoorie is well-maintained. An SUV is recommended for the Mussoorie-Dhanaulti stretch, which can be icy in early mornings.


### 2. Chopta (200 km, ~7 hours)

Called the "Mini Switzerland of India," Chopta receives heavy snowfall from December to February. The trek to Tungnath Temple (3,680m) is a stunning snow trek. The drive is challenging — narrow mountain roads with possible black ice. **SUV with all-terrain tyres is mandatory**. Only experienced mountain drivers should attempt this.


### 3. Auli (296 km, ~10 hours)

India's premier ski resort. Auli receives consistent snowfall from late December through February. The drive from Dehradun via Joshimath is long and demanding in winter. Consider breaking the journey with an overnight stop in Rudraprayag. [Read our Auli road-trip guide](/blog/dehradun-to-auli-road-trip "Dehradun to Auli guide").


### 4. Chakrata (88 km, ~3.5 hours)

A secluded cantonment town that occasionally receives snow in peak winter. Less crowded than Mussoorie but equally beautiful. Tiger Falls is frozen and ethereal in January.


## Snow Driving Safety Essentials

- **Vehicle Choice:** An [SUV with high ground clearance](/browse?category=suv "SUV for winter mountain driving") and fresh tyre tread is absolutely non-negotiable for winter mountain driving.
- **Chains:** Carry tyre chains for extreme snow conditions above 2,500m altitude. Ask our team about chain availability for your rental vehicle.
- **Drive During Daylight Only:** Mountain roads freeze over after sunset. Black ice is invisible and deadly. Start early, arrive early.
- **Low Gears:** Use 2nd gear throughout snowy stretches. Engine braking prevents skidding on icy descents.
- **Fog Awareness:** The Doon Valley gets dense morning fog in winter. Don't start the Mussoorie ascent before the fog lifts (typically by 10 AM).
- **Fuel Up Early:** Mountain fuel stations may have reduced hours in winter. Fill up completely in Dehradun.


## What to Pack for Winter Driving

- Thermal inner layers, warm jacket, gloves, and woollen cap
- Sunglasses (snow glare causes snow blindness at altitude)
- Emergency blanket (in case of breakdowns in extreme cold)
- Hot water in a thermos flask
- Torch/flashlight with extra batteries
- Phone power bank (cold drains batteries faster)


## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to drive to Mussoorie in winter?

Yes, with caution. The Dehradun-Mussoorie road is well-maintained and rarely has snow below the 1,800m mark. However, icy patches can form on the shaded sections of the mountain road in early mornings (before 9 AM). Wait for sun exposure to melt any frost before starting the ascent.

### When does Uttarakhand get snow?

Higher altitude destinations (above 2,000m) like Auli, Chopta, and Dhanaulti receive snow from late December through February. Lower areas like Mussoorie get occasional snow, while Dehradun itself almost never receives snow.


### What's the best winter month for a snow road trip?

Late January to mid-February offers the best snow conditions. December can be cold but dry. March sees snow melting, which creates muddy, slushy roads — less photogenic and harder to drive.
