## Why Drive to Haridwar Instead of Taking a Train?

The Shatabdi Express to Haridwar is fast, but it locks you into fixed schedules and drops you at the railway station — from where you still need local transport. Self-drive advantages:


- **Flexible Departure:** Leave at 3 AM for a sunrise Ganga dip, or after lunch for an evening Aarti arrival.
- **Family Comfort:** Elderly pilgrims and children travel far more comfortably in a private, air-conditioned car than in a train coach.
- **Onward Mobility:** Use the same car to visit Rishikesh (25 km), Rajaji National Park (10 km), or continue to Mussoorie (85 km).
- **Ritual Items:** Carry puja materials, offerings, and prasad securely in the boot without luggage restrictions.

## Essential Pit Stops

- **Delhi-Meerut Expressway Rest Area:** Clean facilities and quick snacks within the first hour.
- **Cheetal Grand, Modipuram:** A popular highway restaurant with diverse food options and clean restrooms.
- **Roorkee:** A good midpoint refueling stop. Multiple petrol stations and local eateries available.
- **Haridwar Bypass Dhabas:** Traditional vegetarian highway food just before entering Haridwar. Fresh chai and aloo parathas.


## Parking in Haridwar

Parking is the biggest challenge in Haridwar, especially during festivals and the Kanwar Yatra season:


- **Municipal Parking (near Har Ki Pauri):** ₹50-100/day. Fills up by 9 AM during peak season.
- **Hotel Parking:** Most hotels along Hari Ki Pauri and Shantikunj Road offer private parking. Confirm at booking.
- **Rahi Motel (GMVN):** Reliable government-run parking on the outskirts. Walk or auto-rickshaw to the ghats.


## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best time to start the Delhi-Haridwar drive?

Starting at 4-5 AM gets you to Haridwar by 9 AM — perfect for a morning Ganga dip. The roads are empty at dawn, making the drive faster and more pleasant.


### Is the route safe during the Kanwar Yatra?

During Kanwar Yatra (July-August), millions of devotees walk along NH334 from Haridwar to Delhi. Traffic is heavily diverted and restricted. Self-drive is possible but expect significant delays and alternative route enforcement. Check traffic advisories before departing.


### Can I continue from Haridwar to Char Dham in the same rental car?

Yes. Our vehicles are all-India permitted. If you're extending to the Char Dham circuit (Badrinath, Kedarnath, Gangotri, Yamunotri), we recommend upgrading to an SUV for the mountain roads beyond Haridwar.


### Are there EV charging stations on the Delhi-Haridwar route?

Yes. Multiple DC fast-charging stations have been installed along NH58/334 at approximately 50 km intervals. However, charging availability can be unpredictable. For a stress-free pilgrimage drive, petrol or diesel vehicles remain more reliable.


### What should I carry for the drive?

Essentials include a valid driving licence, rental agreement printout, FASTag (included in all Brothers rentals), drinking water, light snacks, sunglasses, and if visiting during monsoon, a basic emergency kit with a torch and umbrella.
