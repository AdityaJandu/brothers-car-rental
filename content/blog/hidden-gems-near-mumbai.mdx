Mumbai is a city of relentless energy — which makes the quiet escapes just a few hours' drive away all the more precious. Forget the crowded tourist spots in Lonavala; here are five hidden road-trip gems that most Mumbaikars haven't fully discovered yet.


## 1. Kashid Beach Coastal Drive (130 km, ~3 hours)

While Alibaug gets all the tourist attention, Kashid Beach remains blissfully uncrowded. White sand, clear turquoise water, and casuarina trees line this stunning stretch of the Konkan coastline. The scenic drive down the coastal road is half the experience, especially on weekdays.


## 2. Tikona Fort Trekking Base (120 km, ~3 hours)

A striking triangular hill fort rising sharply from the Sahyadri mountain range. The panoramic views from the top — especially during the monsoon when the valleys fill with rolling clouds — are extraordinary. Secure a vehicle with premium traction control, park at the base village, and hike up.


## 3. Soma Vine Village, Nashik (170 km, ~4 hours)

India's answer to Napa Valley. Tour expansive vineyards, attend exclusive wine-tasting sessions, and stay overnight in a luxury vineyard villa. The Nashik highway corridor is excellent — a comfortable [premium sedan](/browse?category=sedan "Rent a sedan from Mumbai") handles it beautifully.


## 4. Bhandardara Lake District (165 km, ~4 hours)

A deeply peaceful lakeside hamlet nestled in the mountains. Arthur Lake, Wilson Dam, and the summer firefly sanctuaries make this a nature lover's absolute paradise. An [SUV rental is highly recommended](/browse?category=suv "Rent an SUV from Mumbai") for navigating the winding mountain approaches safely.


## 5. Diveagar Beach (170 km, ~4 hours)

A pristine Konkan beach with golden sand and almost no commercialisation. The drive through the Konkan countryside — passing lush paddy fields, palm trees, and laterite villages — is pure driving therapy.
