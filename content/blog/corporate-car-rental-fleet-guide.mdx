Managing a corporate fleet is expensive, time-consuming, and distracting from your core business. Between insurance renewals, scheduled servicing, driver management, depreciation accounting, and the inevitable "my company car has a flat tyre" calls, fleet ownership consumes disproportionate management bandwidth. **Corporate car rental** eliminates all of this with a predictable monthly cost and zero operational overhead.


## Corporate Rental Use Cases

- **Employee mobility programs:** Provide subscription cars to key employees instead of car allowances. They get a new, maintained vehicle; you get predictable costs and no asset risk.
- **Client transport:** Impress clients with clean, premium vehicles for airport pickups, site visits, and business meetings.
- **Project-based fleets:** Construction, event management, and consulting companies need vehicles only during active projects. Subscribe for project duration, return at completion.
- **Sales team mobility:** Equip field sales teams with reliable vehicles without the capital expenditure of buying 10-20 cars.
- **Executive transport:** Premium sedans and SUVs for C-suite executives and board members.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can corporate rentals be used for outstation travel?

Yes. All corporate fleet vehicles carry all-India permits. Employees can use them for client meetings, site visits, and business travel across states.


### What about GST invoicing?

We provide GST-compliant invoices for all corporate rental payments. Your finance team can claim input tax credit on the rental expenses.


### Can we brand the rental cars with our company logo?

For fleets of 16+ vehicles on annual plans, custom magnetic branding (removable) can be arranged. Permanent vehicle wrapping is available on a case-by-case basis.


### Is there a minimum corporate fleet size?

Corporate pricing begins at 3 vehicles. For smaller requirements, our standard monthly subscription plans offer competitive rates.
