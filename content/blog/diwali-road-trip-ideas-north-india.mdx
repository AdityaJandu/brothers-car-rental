Diwali — the festival of lights — is the longest holiday break most working Indians get. With 4-5 days off, a self-drive road trip to a destination that celebrates Diwali in its own unique way transforms a routine festival into an unforgettable experience. Here are **5 Diwali road trip destinations from Delhi** with driving guides and celebration highlights.


## 1. Jaipur — The Pink City Diwali (280 km, 5 hrs)

Jaipur during Diwali is otherworldly. The entire walled city is illuminated with thousands of earthen diyas and electric lights. Nahargarh Fort, Hawa Mahal, and Amber Fort glow against the dark sky. The bazaars explode with firework shopping, sweets, and festive energy.


- **Must-see:** Hawa Mahal illumination, Nahargarh Fort viewpoint at night, Johari Bazaar firecrackers
- **Best car:** [Sedan](/browse?category=sedan "Sedan for Jaipur Diwali") on NH48 expressway
- **Book early:** Jaipur hotels sell out 3-4 weeks before Diwali


## 2. Varanasi — The Spiritual Diwali (850 km, 12-14 hrs or fly + rent)

Dev Deepawali — the Diwali of the Gods — occurs 15 days after Diwali in Varanasi. Over 1 million earthen lamps illuminate the ghats of the Ganga in an unforgettable spectacle of devotion and light.


- **Must-see:** Dashashwamedh Ghat illumination, Ganga boat ride during lamp ceremony
- **Pro tip:** Fly to Varanasi and rent an [airport car](/browse?category=hatchback "Budget car in Varanasi") — the drive from Delhi is very long


## 3. Rishikesh — The Peaceful Diwali (240 km, 5.5 hrs)

While Delhi chokes on firework smoke, Rishikesh offers a clean, peaceful Diwali by the Ganga. Ashrams host special prayers, the ghats are lit with floating diyas, and the mountain air is refreshingly clear.


- **Must-see:** Ganga Aarti enhanced for Diwali, floating diya ceremony
- **Best car:** Any category — flat highway drive
- **Bonus:** Escape Delhi's post-Diwali pollution for 2-3 days of mountain-fresh air


## 4. Udaipur — The Lakeside Diwali (660 km, 10-11 hrs)

Udaipur's Diwali celebrations transform the City of Lakes into a floating constellation. Lake Pichola reflects thousands of lights from the surrounding palaces and temples. The City Palace illumination is spectacular.


- **Must-see:** City Palace illumination, Lake Pichola boat ride, Jagdish Temple celebrations
- **Best car:** [SUV](/browse?category=suv "SUV for Udaipur road trip") (long drive with some Aravalli hill sections)
- **Break the journey:** Overnight in Ajmer or Pushkar en route


## 5. Mussoorie — The Mountain Diwali (290 km, 6.5 hrs)

A Diwali in the mountains is magical — clear skies mean the Milky Way is visible between firework bursts, and Mall Road twinkles with festive lights against the backdrop of snow-capped Himalayan peaks.


- **Must-see:** Mall Road lights, valley view fireworks from Gun Hill
- **Best car:** [SUV](/browse?category=suv "SUV for Mussoorie Diwali") for the mountain ascent
- **Note:** Mussoorie can get cold during Diwali (October/November) — pack warm layers


## Diwali Driving Tips

- **Expect heavy traffic:** The Diwali weekend sees massive highway traffic as people travel to hometowns. Start before dawn or travel on off-peak days.
- **Firework hazards:** Children throwing fireworks near roads is common in towns and villages. Drive slowly through populated areas, especially after dark.
- **Book rental cars early:** Diwali weekend is peak demand. Book your car 3-4 weeks in advance to guarantee availability.
- **Return post-Diwali traffic:** The drive back to Delhi on the last day of the long weekend is brutal. Consider extending by one day to avoid the crush.
