## The NCR Mobility Challenge

NCR's unique geography creates a transportation puzzle that no single solution solves. The Delhi Metro covers the core urban corridors well, but falls short for:


- **Cross-border commutes:** Moving between Gurgaon (Haryana), Noida (UP), and Delhi involves crossing state boundaries, each with different auto-rickshaw unions and variable cab availability.
- **Industrial zone access:** Manufacturing hubs in Manesar, Bhiwadi, Greater Noida, and Faridabad are poorly connected by public transport.
- **Airport logistics:** Reaching IGI Airport from Noida or Ghaziabad during peak hours is a nightmare with ride-hailing surge pricing.
- **Weekend escapes:** NCR's 30+ million residents create massive demand for weekend getaway transportation. Booking a cab to Agra or Shimla on a Friday evening means surge prices 3-4x normal.

A self-drive rental solves every one of these scenarios at a predictable, flat daily rate.
