## The Drive Experience

Leaving Haridwar's busy temple area, the road quickly enters a serene stretch flanked by the Rajaji National Park forest on one side and the Ganga on the other. This corridor is known for occasional elephant crossings — drive slowly through the forest stretch and watch for wildlife warning signs.


- **Chilla Barrage:** A dam across the Ganga visible from the road. The reservoir is popular for birdwatching during winter months.
- **Rajaji Forest Stretch (5 km):** Dense sal forest on both sides. Reduce speed to 30 km/h — elephants, deer, and peacocks have been spotted crossing here.
- **Rishikesh Entry:** The road descends into Rishikesh past the AIIMS campus. Follow signs for Ram Jhula/Laxman Jhula.


## What to See Between Haridwar and Rishikesh

- **Chandi Devi Ropeway (Haridwar):** Start with this cable car to Chandi Devi Temple before driving to Rishikesh.
- **Rajaji Wildlife Spotting:** During the forest stretch, keep an eye out for langurs in the trees and peafowl on the roadside.
- **Triveni Ghat (Rishikesh):** Your destination for the evening Ganga Aarti. Park nearby and walk to the ghat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Haridwar-Rishikesh road safe at night?

The road passes through Rajaji forest corridor where elephants may be on the road after dark. Avoid this stretch after sunset. The urban sections at both ends are safe.


### Is there parking at Rishikesh ghats?

Limited parking near Triveni Ghat and Ram Jhula. Arrive before 10 AM for best availability. Municipal parking lots charge ₹50-100/day.


### Can I visit both cities in a day trip from Dehradun?

Absolutely. Dehradun → Haridwar (55 km) → Rishikesh (25 km) → Dehradun (43 km) is a comfortable 120 km loop that covers both sacred cities in a single day. [Read our Rishikesh day trip guide](/blog/dehradun-to-rishikesh-day-trip "Dehradun to Rishikesh day trip").


### Is Kanwar Yatra season safe for this drive?

During Kanwar Yatra (July-August), the Haridwar-Rishikesh road is heavily congested with pilgrims walking. Traffic restrictions may be in place. Check police advisories before driving during this period.
