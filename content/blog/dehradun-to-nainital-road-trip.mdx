## Route Guide

### Dehradun → Haridwar (55 km, ~1.5 hours)

A familiar and comfortable drive through the Doon Valley foothills via NH7.


### Haridwar → Najibabad → Kashipur (140 km, ~3 hours)

This stretch takes you through the Terai plains along the base of the Shivalik foothills. The road passes through Bijnor and Najibabad — both are good fuel and food stops. Watch for heavy truck traffic on this corridor.


### Kashipur → Haldwani (30 km, ~45 min)

Haldwani is the last major city before the Nainital ascent. This is your final chance to fuel up, grab supplies, and stretch your legs before the mountain drive begins.


### Haldwani → Nainital (35 km, ~1.5 hours)

The mountain section. The road climbs sharply from the plains (400m) to Nainital (2,084m) through dense forests and hairpin bends. The gradient is steep — use second gear liberally. An [SUV's torque and ground clearance](/browse?category=suv "SUV for Nainital hills") make a noticeable difference here.


## Alternative: Via Bhimtal and Naukuchiatal

Instead of driving directly to Nainital, consider the scenic detour through Bhimtal and Naukuchiatal — two gorgeous lakes that are less crowded than Naini Lake. From Haldwani, take the Bhimtal road (24 km), enjoy the lake, continue to Naukuchiatal (6 km), and then drive to Nainital (22 km). This adds 30 minutes but offers a richer lake-hopping experience.


## Best Car for Dehradun to Nainital

## Nainital Parking & Tips

- **Mall Road is restricted:** Private vehicles are banned on Nainital's Mall Road. Park at the Tallital or Mallital parking areas and walk.
- **Parking fees:** ₹50-100/day at municipal lots. Hotels with private parking are ideal — confirm at booking.
- **Boat Ride:** Naini Lake boat rides are a must. ₹200-400 for a 30-minute paddle boat or rowboat session.


## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long is the drive from Dehradun to Nainital?

Plan for 7-8 hours of driving time. With stops, the total journey takes 8-9 hours. Starting early (6 AM) ensures arrival by mid-afternoon.


### Is the Dehradun-Nainital route safe for self-drive?

The highway portion is straightforward. The Haldwani-Nainital mountain section requires careful driving — steep gradients, hairpin bends, and occasional tourist traffic. Experienced drivers handle it comfortably; beginners should drive slowly and avoid peak hours.


### Can I rent a car in Dehradun and drop it in Nainital?

### What is the best season for this drive?

March to June (spring/summer) offers pleasant weather and clear mountain views. September to November (post-monsoon) provides crisp, clean air. Avoid July-August when heavy rains can cause landslides on the Nainital road.


### Can I continue from Nainital to other Kumaon lakes?

Absolutely. Nainital is the hub for exploring Bhimtal, Sattal, Naukuchiatal, and Mukteshwar — all within 20-40 km. Your rental car makes this multi-lake circuit effortless.
