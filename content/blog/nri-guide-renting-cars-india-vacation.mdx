## Can NRIs Rent a Car in India?

Absolutely yes. NRIs (both Indian passport holders and foreign nationals of Indian origin) can rent self-drive cars in India. The process is straightforward:


- **Indian Passport Holders:** Your Indian driving licence (if still valid) is accepted directly. If your Indian licence has expired, an International Driving Permit (IDP) paired with your foreign licence is accepted.
- **Foreign Passport Holders (OCI/PIO):** An International Driving Permit (IDP) is required along with your home country driving licence.

## Driving in India: What NRIs Need to Know

If you've been driving abroad for years, Indian roads will feel like controlled chaos. Here's what's different:


### 1. Left-Hand Traffic

India drives on the left side of the road. If you've been driving in the US, UAE, or Europe (right-hand traffic countries), this takes conscious adjustment for the first hour. After that, muscle memory kicks in.


### 2. The Unwritten Rules

- **Honking is communication, not aggression:** Indian drivers honk to say "I'm here" at blind intersections, while overtaking, and near pedestrians. It's expected — don't be offended or hesitant to honk.
- **Lane discipline is aspirational:** Lanes exist on paper but are routinely ignored, especially in city traffic. Watch the vehicle ahead and around you, not lane markings.
- **Right of way goes to the bigger vehicle:** In practice, buses and trucks take priority through sheer size. Don't assert right-of-way against a bus — physics wins.
- **Speed bumps everywhere:** Unmarked speed bumps appear without warning in residential areas, villages, and school zones. Slow down in any populated area.


### 3. Choose Automatic Transmission

If you've been driving automatics abroad (which most NRIs have), don't switch to manual for your India trip. City traffic involves constant clutch work that will exhaust you. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use my US/UK/UAE driving licence directly in India?

No. A foreign driving licence alone is not legally valid for driving in India. You need an International Driving Permit (IDP) obtained in your country of residence before travelling to India. The IDP is valid for 1 year.


### Is self-driving safe for NRIs unfamiliar with Indian roads?

For city driving — it takes adjustment but is manageable. For highway driving (expressways) — it's actually quite comfortable and similar to driving abroad. For mountain roads — only if you're comfortable with the techniques. Start with highways before attempting mountains.


### Can I pay in foreign currency?

All payments are processed in Indian Rupees (INR). International credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) are accepted. Your bank will handle the currency conversion at their prevailing exchange rate.


### What if I get into an accident?

Call our 24/7 support line immediately. Do not leave the scene. Take photos. Exchange information with the other party. Our insurance covers all vehicles — you're protected from the moment you pick up the car.


### Do I need an Indian phone number?

An Indian phone number is helpful for OTP verifications, support calls, and Google Maps. You can get a prepaid SIM at the airport (Jio or Airtel) with your passport and OCI card. International roaming works but is more expensive.
