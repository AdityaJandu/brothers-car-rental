Dehradun is the perfect launchpad for some of North India's most unforgettable road trips. With well-maintained highways connecting you to the Garhwal Himalayas, vast deserts, and heritage cities, all you need is the right **rental car**. Here are our top five driving routes starting from Dehradun.

## 1. Dehradun to Shimla by Car (350 km, ~7 hours)

The classic hill station escape. Wind through stunning pine forests on NH44 and NH5, stopping at Pinjore Gardens and the charming town of Solan along the way. **Best car to rent:** A mid-size SUV handles the sharp mountain curves with ease — [browse our SUV rental range](/browse?category=suv "Rent an SUV for mountain roads").

## 2. Dehradun to Jaipur Road Trip (280 km, ~5 hours)

The majestic Pink City is a straight shot down NH48. Visit the Amber Fort, explore Johari Bazaar, and feast on local Rajasthani cuisine. A comfortable, fuel-efficient sedan makes this flat highway drive an absolute breeze.

## 3. Dehradun to Rishikesh Drive (250 km, ~6 hours)

For the spiritually inclined or the adventure seeker, Rishikesh is unmatched. It offers world-class yoga retreats, white-water rafting, and the iconic Lakshman Jhula. The driving route via Haridwar is highly scenic and perfectly paved.

## 4. Dehradun to Agra via Yamuna Expressway (230 km, ~4 hours)

The world-class Yamuna Expressway makes this one of the fastest weekend getaways from Dehradun. See the Taj Mahal at sunrise and be back home by dinner. Perfect for a day trip in a [comfortable rental sedan](/browse?category=sedan "Rent a sedan for highway driving").

## 5. Dehradun to Jim Corbett National Park (260 km, ~6 hours)

India's oldest national park is a wildlife lover's ultimate dream. Spot Bengal tigers, wild elephants, and rare birds. An SUV car hire is strictly essential for navigating the unpaved forest and buffer zone roads safely.

## Driving Tips for Himalayan Road Trips

- **Start Early:** Aim to leave Dehradun before 6 AM to beat local traffic.
- **Keep Documents Handy:** Have your original driving licence and rental agreement ready at toll plazas.
- **Offline Maps:** Download offline GPS maps — cellular network coverage drops frequently in high-altitude mountains.
