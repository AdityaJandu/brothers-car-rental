## Age & Licence Requirements

- **Minimum age:** 21 years with a valid driving licence held for 1+ year. Most 3rd/4th year students and all postgraduate students qualify.
- **Under 21?** A friend or senior who is 21+ can be the primary renter. You can drive as an authorized additional driver on some plans.
- **Learner's licence:** Not accepted. You need a full permanent licence.

## Student Money-Saving Tips

- **Split everything 4 ways:** Rental, fuel, tolls, parking — divide by 4 and the per-head cost becomes remarkably cheap.
- **Book 2 weeks early:** Last-minute weekend bookings cost 15-25% more. Plan ahead.
- **Choose manual over automatic:** 15-25% cheaper per day. College students are typically comfortable with manual transmission.
- **Dhaba meals, not restaurants:** Highway dhabas serve filling meals for ₹100-150/person. Save restaurant budgets for the destination.
- **Return with full tank:** If you don't, the rental company charges inflated refueling rates. Fill up at the nearest pump before returning.
- **Weekday trips:** If your schedule allows, Tuesday-Thursday rentals are 10-15% cheaper than Friday-Sunday.

Yes — Brothers vehicles have all-India permits. Delhi to Goa is approximately 1,900 km (24 hours of driving over 3 days). A group of 4-5 in an SUV makes this an epic and affordable adventure. Budget approximately ₹5,000-6,000 per head for the round trip (rental + fuel + tolls).
