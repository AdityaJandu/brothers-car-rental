Kedarnath — one of the twelve Jyotirlingas and a key stop on the Char Dham Yatra — sits at 3,583 metres in the Garhwal Himalayas. While the final 16 km trek from Gaurikund to the temple must be done on foot (or by helicopter/pony), the **drive from Delhi or Dehradun to Sonprayag/Gaurikund** is an epic mountain road trip through some of Uttarakhand's most dramatic landscapes.

## Day-by-Day Itinerary (from Delhi)

### Day 1: Delhi → Rudraprayag (380 km)

Start at 4 AM to beat Delhi traffic. NH58 to Haridwar, then mountain roads via Devprayag. Stop at **Devprayag** to witness the sacred confluence of Alaknanda and Bhagirathi rivers — the birthplace of the Ganga. Arrive Rudraprayag by evening.


### Day 2: Rudraprayag → Sonprayag (80 km)

A dramatic mountain drive through the Mandakini River valley. The road climbs steadily through Agastmuni, Guptkashi, and Phata. Park at Sonprayag — the last motorable point before the trek. Private vehicles are not allowed beyond Sonprayag; shared jeeps shuttle to Gaurikund (5 km).


### Day 3: Gaurikund → Kedarnath Trek (16 km, 6-8 hrs)

The trek begins at Gaurikund (1,982m) and ascends to Kedarnath Temple (3,583m). Options: walk, pony ride, or helicopter. Stay overnight at Kedarnath.


### Day 4: Return Trek + Drive to Rudraprayag

Descend to Gaurikund, jeep to Sonprayag, collect your car, and drive back to Rudraprayag for rest.


### Day 5: Rudraprayag → Delhi/Dehradun

Relaxed return drive. Consider stopping at Rishikesh for evening Ganga Aarti before the final stretch to Delhi.


## Vehicle Requirement

An [SUV is mandatory](/browse?category=suv "SUV for Kedarnath road trip"). The Rudraprayag-Sonprayag road includes steep gradients, narrow sections with sheer drops, and possible landslide debris during early season (May-June). Diesel SUVs with strong torque are preferred for sustained mountain climbing.


## Essential Tips

- **Registration:** Biometric registration at Sonprayag is mandatory for all Kedarnath pilgrims.
- **Fuel:** Fill up at Rudraprayag — last reliable fuel station. Guptkashi has a small pump but it occasionally runs dry during peak season.
- **Cash:** Carry ₹10,000+ in cash. ATMs and UPI are unreliable beyond Rudraprayag.
- **Altitude sickness:** Kedarnath's 3,583m altitude can cause headaches and breathlessness. Ascend slowly, stay hydrated, carry Diamox if prescribed.
- **Parking at Sonprayag:** Guarded parking lots charge ₹200-300/day. Your rental car will be safe for 2-3 days.


## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is Kedarnath open for darshan?

The temple opens in late April/early May (Akshaya Tritiya) and closes in November (Bhai Dooj). The best driving weather is May-June and September-October.


### Is the helicopter a good alternative to trekking?

Yes. Helicopter services operate from Phata, Guptkashi, and Sirsi to Kedarnath. The flight takes 10 minutes and costs ₹7,000-12,000 per person. Book well in advance during peak season.


### Can I drive beyond Sonprayag?

No. Private vehicles are prohibited beyond Sonprayag. Government-operated shared jeeps shuttle pilgrims from Sonprayag to Gaurikund (₹50-100/person). Walk the last few km if you prefer.


### Is the road safe during monsoon?

The Rudraprayag-Kedarnath road is one of Uttarakhand's most landslide-prone routes during monsoon (July-August). Avoid this route during peak monsoon unless you check SDRF road status daily and are prepared for multi-hour delays.
