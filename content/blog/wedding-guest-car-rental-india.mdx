## Why Rent a Car for Wedding Travel?

- **Unlimited luggage:** Lehengas, sherwanis, sarees, and jewellery boxes fill a sedan's boot easily. No airline weight restrictions, no train berth space fights.
- **Arrive in style:** Pull up to the wedding venue in a clean, polished sedan or SUV. First impressions matter at Indian weddings.
- **Event mobility:** Wedding functions span 3-4 days across multiple venues (mehndi, sangeet, wedding, reception). Having your own car means no dependency on shared transport between events.
- **Family comfort:** Transport elderly relatives, children, and the entire family together in air-conditioned comfort between hotel and venue.
- **Late-night returns:** Wedding functions end late (1-2 AM). Finding cabs at that hour is unreliable. Your rental car is always ready.

## Wedding Season Booking Tips

- **Book 3-4 weeks early:** November-February and April-June are peak wedding seasons. Rental car demand spikes for auspicious dates. Early booking guarantees your preferred vehicle.
- **Choose a dark-coloured car:** Silver, black, or dark blue cars photograph better at wedding venues than white or light colours. Request a colour preference at booking if possible.
- **Return timing:** Wedding dates are fixed but reception timings aren't. Book an extra half-day buffer to avoid late-return penalties.
