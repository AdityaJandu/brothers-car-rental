## When Airport Rental Wins

- **Multi-day trips (3+ days):** The daily rental cost is fixed. Taxi costs accumulate with every ride.
- **Outstation plans:** Flying into Delhi and driving to Jaipur, Agra, or Rishikesh? Rental is the only practical option.
- **Flexible schedule:** No waiting for Uber surges, cancellations, or driver availability. Your car is always ready.
- **Privacy and comfort:** Your own car, your music, your AC setting, your conversation privacy.
- **Early morning / late night:** Taxi availability and surge pricing at odd hours make rental more reliable and predictable.


## When Taxi/Uber Wins

- **Single airport transfer:** If you just need to get from the airport to your hotel and back, a taxi is simpler and cheaper.
- **1-day trips:** For a single day with only 2-3 trips, individual cab rides may cost less than a full-day rental.
- **Unfamiliar city driving:** If you're uncomfortable with the city's traffic, a taxi driver who knows the routes has value.
- **Alcohol at dinner:** If your evening plans include drinks, having a taxi option means no one drives impaired.


## The Hybrid Approach

Smart travellers combine both: rent a car for outstation driving and day explorations, then use Uber for evening outings where parking is difficult or alcohol is involved. This gives you the best of both worlds.

### What about parking costs with a rental?

Factor in ₹100-300/day for parking at hotels and tourist sites. This is a real cost that taxis avoid. However, having your car parked at your hotel gives 24/7 departure flexibility that taxis cannot match.


### Is surge pricing really that bad?

During peak hours, airport rush, rain, and late nights, Uber/Ola surge pricing can multiply fares by 1.5-3x. A rental car has zero surge pricing — your daily rate is fixed regardless of demand, weather, or time.
