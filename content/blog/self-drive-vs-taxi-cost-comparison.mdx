## When Self-Drive Wins Decisively

- **Multi-day trips:** Driver allowances and night charges add ₹500-800/day to taxi costs. Self-drive eliminates this entirely.
- **Groups of 3+:** Split the rental cost between 3-4 travellers and self-drive becomes dramatically cheaper per person than individual cab fares.
- **Local sightseeing:** Taxis charge per-kilometre rates for local exploration. Your rental car covers unlimited local driving at zero extra cost.

## When Taxis Make More Sense

- **Solo airport transfers:** A quick, one-way airport pickup is cheaper as a single cab ride than a full-day car rental.
- **Unfamiliar city driving:** If you're visiting Mumbai or Kolkata for the first time and are uncomfortable with aggressive city traffic, a cab driver who knows the routes is valuable.
- **Business backseat work:** Need to take Zoom calls and review documents during transit? A chauffeur lets you use the travel time productively.


## The Hidden Costs of Ride-Hailing Apps

Ola and Uber advertise low base fares, but the real cost often looks very different:


- **Surge Pricing:** During peak hours, rain, or high demand, fares can triple. A ₹300 ride becomes ₹900 without warning.
- **Cancellation Roulette:** Drivers cancel your ride after seeing the destination, wasting 15-20 minutes each time. Self-drive means your car is always ready.
- **Route Manipulation:** Some drivers take longer routes to inflate the fare. With self-drive, you pick the fastest route on Google Maps.
- **Outstation Refusal:** App cabs routinely refuse outstation trips or demand off-meter cash payments. A rental car goes anywhere, anytime.


## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is self-drive cheaper than Uber for daily commuting?

For daily commuting (2+ rides/day for a week), a weekly rental at ₹699-999/day is almost always cheaper than two ₹400-600 Uber rides per day, especially with surge pricing factored in.


### What about the cost of fuel in self-drive?

Yes, you pay for fuel separately in self-drive. But this is transparent — you fill at market rate petrol pumps. Taxis embed fuel costs into higher per-km rates, making the per-km cost actually higher than self-fuelling.


### Do I save money on insurance with self-drive?

All Brothers Car Rental vehicles include comprehensive insurance in the daily rate. There's no extra insurance cost. Taxis may or may not carry adequate passenger insurance — it's a risk you're unknowingly taking.


### What about wear and tear on the rental car?

Normal wear and tear is fully covered in your rental agreement. You're not liable for brake pad wear, tyre aging, or engine maintenance. You only pay for rental days and fuel.


### Is self-drive worth it for a solo traveller?
