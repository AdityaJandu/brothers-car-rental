Renting a self-drive car for the first time in India can feel overwhelming. Different rental companies have different policies, Indian traffic is notoriously chaotic, and the documentation process seems complicated. But it doesn't have to be stressful. Here are **15 essential tips for first-time car renters in India** that will make your experience smooth, affordable, and enjoyable.


## Before Booking

### 1. Choose the Right Vehicle Category

Don't rent an SUV if you're driving solo within Delhi. Don't rent a hatchback if you're taking a family to Mussoorie. Match the car category to your trip type: [hatchback for city](/browse?category=hatchback "Budget hatchback"), [sedan for highway](/browse?category=sedan "Highway sedan"), [SUV for mountains](/browse?category=suv "Mountain SUV").


### 2. Book 1-2 Weeks in Advance

Early booking gets you the best vehicle selection and lowest prices. Last-minute bookings (especially for weekends and holidays) may limit your choices and cost 15-25% more.


### 3. Read the Fuel Policy

Full-to-Full is the fairest fuel policy — you get a full tank and return it full. Understand this before pickup so you're not caught off-guard. [Read our fuel policy guide](/blog/car-rental-fuel-policy-explained "Fuel policy guide").


## At Pickup

### 4. Photograph the Car Before Driving

This is the single most important tip. Walk around the car with the delivery agent and photograph every existing scratch, dent, or mark. These photos are your proof that pre-existing damage was not caused by you. Take photos of all four sides, roof, bumpers, and wheels.


### 5. Check Spare Tyre and Tools

Before driving off, verify that the car has a functional spare tyre, a jack, and a wheel wrench. Also check that the first-aid kit is present. You don't want to discover a missing spare on a mountain road at night.


### 6. Adjust Mirrors and Seat Before Starting

Spend 2 minutes adjusting the rearview mirror, both side mirrors, and your seat/steering position. Being physically comfortable from the first kilometre prevents fatigue-related mistakes.


## During the Rental

### 7. Follow Traffic Rules — Seriously

Rental cars are tracked. Speed camera challans, red-light violations, and parking fines will be forwarded to you after the rental. Drive legally — it's cheaper than paying fines.


### 8. Keep All Fuel Receipts

Fuel receipts prove you filled up before return. In case of any fuel-level disputes at drop-off, your receipts are evidence. They also help track your trip expenses.


### 9. Use the FASTag Lane at Toll Plazas

All Brothers vehicles have active FASTags. Use the FASTag lane (usually the leftmost) for automated toll deduction. The cash lane may charge double for FASTag vehicles. [Read our toll guide](/blog/toll-charges-fastag-rental-car-guide "FASTag guide").


### 10. Don't Smoke in the Car

Most rental agreements prohibit smoking inside the vehicle. Violations result in a cleaning charge deducted from your security deposit (typically ₹2,000-5,000). Use designated smoking areas at rest stops.


### 11. Report Problems Immediately

If you hear an unusual noise, notice a warning light, or have any mechanical concern — call 24/7 roadside assistance immediately. Don't "wait and see." Early reporting prevents small issues from becoming expensive problems.


## At Return

### 12. Refuel Before Returning

Fill the tank at the nearest petrol station before the drop-off point. Returning with less than a full tank triggers a refueling charge at inflated rates. Your tank is full, your deposit stays full.


### 13. Return On Time

Late returns incur per-hour charges. If you're running late, call ahead to officially extend the booking — extension charges are usually lower than late-return penalties.


### 14. Be Present for the Return Inspection

Stay with the car during the walkaround inspection. If any new damage is identified, you can discuss it on the spot. Leaving before inspection means accepting whatever findings the team documents.


### 15. Ask About Your Deposit Refund Timeline

Before leaving, confirm the refund timeline for your security deposit. UPI refunds take 24-48 hours; card refunds take 5-10 days. Knowing the timeline sets proper expectations. [Read our deposit guide](/blog/security-deposit-car-rental-guide "Deposit guide").


## Frequently Asked Questions

### What documents do I need for my first rental?

Driving licence (held for 1+ year), Aadhaar card or passport, and a digital payment method (UPI or card). DigiLocker documents are accepted. [See our complete document checklist](/blog/documents-needed-to-rent-a-car "Document checklist").


### Is there a minimum rental period?

Our minimum rental is 8 hours — perfect for day trips. There's no maximum limit; you can rent for days, weeks, or months.


### What if I damage the car?

Minor scratches from normal driving are covered as wear and tear. For more significant damage, your security deposit covers the repair cost. Comprehensive protection plans eliminate excess liability entirely.


### Can I add a second driver?

Yes. Additional drivers can be added to the booking. They must meet the same age and licence requirements as the primary renter.


### What's the most common first-timer mistake?

Not photographing the car at pickup. Without pre-rental photos, you have no evidence that existing damage wasn't caused by you. Always, always photograph the car before driving away.
