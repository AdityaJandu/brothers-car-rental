## How Airport Pickup Works

1. **Book online before your flight:** Select "Delhi Airport" as your pickup location and enter your flight number and landing time. [Book your airport car now](/browse "Book airport car rental Delhi").
2. **Land and clear baggage:** Collect your luggage and clear customs (if international).
3. **Meet our delivery agent:** You'll receive a WhatsApp message with the exact pickup location and agent contact. Walk to the designated meeting point.
4. **Quick handover (10 min):** Verify your ID, sign the digital agreement, do a quick walkaround, and receive the keys.
5. **Drive away:** Navigate out of the airport using Google Maps. Follow NH48 toward Delhi city or any outward route.

## Airport Driving Tips

- **Exiting IGI:** Follow the green "City" signs. The airport exit road connects to NH48 (toward Gurgaon) and the Airport Express Metro line road (toward Dwarka/South Delhi).
- **Avoid rush hours:** Delhi airport approach roads are heavily congested 8-10 AM and 5-8 PM. If your flight lands during these hours, factor in 30-45 minutes of slow traffic getting out.
- **FASTag ready:** Your rental car's FASTag handles the airport parking fee automatically. No cash needed.


## Popular Routes from Delhi Airport

- **To Gurgaon (30 km, 45 min):** Straight down NH48. The nearest major city from the airport.
- **To Central Delhi (20 km, 1 hour):** Via Dhaula Kuan and Ring Road. Heavy traffic most hours.
- **To Noida/Greater Noida (45 km, 1.5 hours):** Via DND Flyway or Noida Expressway.
- **To Agra (230 km, 3.5 hours):** Take Noida Expressway → Yamuna Expressway. [Read the Agra guide](/blog/delhi-to-agra-road-trip "Delhi to Agra by car").

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I return the car at Delhi Airport?

Yes. Airport returns follow the reverse process — drive to the terminal parking, meet our agent, complete the walkaround inspection, and hand over the keys. We handle the parking exit.


### What if my flight is delayed?

We monitor your flight status in real-time. If your flight is delayed, your pickup slot automatically adjusts. No need to call us — we'll be there when you land.


### Can I pick up at T1 and return at T3?

Yes. Cross-terminal pickup and return is available. Specify both terminals during booking so we can arrange accordingly.


### Is there a midnight/late-night surcharge?

Our standard airport pickup operates 6 AM to 10 PM. Late-night pickups (10 PM to 6 AM) may carry a small surcharge for agent deployment. Check at booking.


### Can NRIs pick up a rental car at Delhi Airport?

Absolutely. Bring your International Driving Permit, passport, and OCI card. [Read our NRI rental guide](/blog/nri-guide-renting-cars-india-vacation "NRI car rental guide").
