## How Speed Enforcement Works

- **Fixed Speed Cameras:** Permanently installed on expressways and national highways. They photograph your number plate if you exceed the limit. E-challans are generated automatically.
- **Mobile Speed Guns:** Highway patrol officers with handheld radar guns. They can stop you on the spot.
- **Average Speed Cameras:** Increasingly common on expressways. These calculate your average speed between two points — so slowing down only at camera locations doesn't help.
- **In-Car GPS Tracking:** Some rental companies track vehicle speed via GPS. Brothers Car Rental does not penalize based on GPS speed data, but we may contact you if consistently dangerous speeds are detected.

## Optimal Speeds for Rental Cars

- **Expressway:** 100-110 km/h — safe, legal, and fuel-efficient. The 120 km/h limit leaves no margin for camera error.
- **National Highway:** 80-90 km/h — accounts for trucks, speed bumps in villages, and animal crossings.
- **Mountain Roads:** 30-40 km/h — hairpin bends, blind curves, and steep gradients demand slow, controlled driving.
- **City:** Follow the flow of traffic, typically 40-50 km/h. Avoid exceeding 60 km/h even on wide city roads — cameras are active.


## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will I know if I get a speed camera challan during my rental?

Speed camera e-challans are generated within 24-48 hours but may take 1-2 weeks to be processed and linked to the vehicle. If a challan is issued during your rental period, we'll notify you and deduct the fine from your security deposit.


### What if the speed limit sign is unclear or missing?

Default to 100 km/h on 4-lane highways, 80 km/h on 2-lane roads, and 50 km/h in urban areas. These are the Motor Vehicles Act defaults when no specific sign is posted.


### Are speed limits different for different vehicle types?

Yes. The limits above are for cars (LMV). Trucks and buses have lower limits (typically 60-80 km/h on highways). Some expressways have separate speed limits for different vehicle classes — always check posted signs.


### Can I fight a speed camera challan?

You can challenge e-challans through the state traffic police portal within 30 days. However, speed camera evidence (timestamped photos with speed reading) is usually incontrovertible. It's easier and cheaper to just drive within limits.
