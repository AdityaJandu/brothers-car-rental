## The Verdict: Renting Saves ₹5,000-15,000/Month

When you factor in depreciation, insurance, maintenance, and the opportunity cost of the down payment locked up in a depreciating asset, monthly car rental comes out significantly cheaper for most urban Indians. And that's before considering the flexibility advantages:


- **No Down Payment:** Skip the ₹2-4 lakh down payment. Keep that capital invested and earning returns.
- **Zero Depreciation Worry:** The car's value decline is our problem, not yours.
- **Swap Vehicles:** Need an SUV for a family trip? Swap your hatchback for a week. Buying doesn't offer this flexibility.
- **City Transfers:** Moving to a new city? Simply return the car and rent locally. No RTO transfer nightmares.
- **No Resale Hassle:** When you're done, you simply return the keys. No dealing with used car dealers or marketplace scammers.


## When Buying Still Makes Sense

- **You drive 50,000+ km/year:** Very high-mileage users may find ownership marginally cheaper per kilometre.
- **Emotional attachment:** If owning a specific dream car matters more than financial optimization, buy it.
- **Rural/tier-3 cities:** Where rental infrastructure isn't available, ownership remains the only option.
- **Business asset:** Certain businesses can claim depreciation benefits on owned vehicles.
