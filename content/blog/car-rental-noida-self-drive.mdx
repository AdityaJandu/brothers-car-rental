## Driving Tips Specific to Noida

- **DND Flyway Tolls:** The Delhi-Noida Direct Flyway charges ₹30-40 per crossing. FASTag is mandatory — all our cars come FASTag-equipped.
- **Sector Navigation:** Noida sectors follow a grid pattern. Even-numbered sectors are generally on one side of the main road, odd on the other. Once you understand the grid, navigation becomes intuitive.
- **Speed Limits:** The Noida Expressway has a strict 100 km/h limit with active speed cameras. The Yamuna Expressway allows 120 km/h for cars.
- **Avoid U-Turn Traps:** Many sector roads require long U-turns. Use Google Maps for the shortest legal route.
