## Step-by-Step Route Guide

1. **Delhi → Meerut (70 km, ~1 hour):** The Delhi-Meerut Expressway gets you out of the city in record time. 14-lane glory.
2. **Meerut → Muzaffarnagar (65 km, ~1 hour):** Continue on NH58/NH334 through flat farmland. The road is wide and well-maintained.
3. **Muzaffarnagar → Roorkee (50 km, ~45 min):** Sugar mill country. The landscape starts subtly changing as you approach the Shivalik foothills.
4. **Roorkee → Haridwar (30 km, ~30 min):** The first glimpse of the Shivalik hills appears. Traffic increases near Haridwar — go slow through the city.
5. **Haridwar → Rishikesh (25 km, ~30 min):** A beautiful riverside drive along the Ganga canal. The road hugs the river with stunning views. [Read our detailed Haridwar-Rishikesh guide](/blog/haridwar-to-rishikesh-self-drive "Haridwar to Rishikesh by car").

## Things to Do in Rishikesh by Car

Having your own wheels in Rishikesh transforms the experience:


- **Shivpuri Rafting (16 km from Rishikesh):** Drive to the rafting put-in point at your chosen time. No shared transport dependency.
- **Neelkanth Mahadev Temple (30 km):** A sacred mountain drive through dense forest. Limited public transport — your own car is essential.
- **Beatles Ashram:** Park near the ashram entrance (parking available) and explore at your leisure.
- **Triveni Ghat Evening Aarti:** Drive down to the ghat for the mesmerizing 6 PM Ganga Aarti and return to your hotel in comfort.
- **Patna Waterfall (20 km):** A hidden gem accessible only by personal vehicle. The last 5 km is a forest road — park and trek the final 500 metres.


## Pit Stops & Food on the Route

- **Cheetal Grand, Roorkee:** The best highway restaurant on this route. Clean washrooms, fast food, and a mini convenience store.
- **Haridwar Dhabas:** Stop for a quick chai and samosa near the Haridwar bypass. The Kankhal area has excellent vegetarian dhabas.
- **Chotiwala, Rishikesh:** The legendary vegetarian restaurant at Ram Jhula. A must-visit once you arrive.


## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long is the drive from Delhi to Rishikesh?

The drive takes 5-5.5 hours via NH334 through Meerut, Muzaffarnagar, Roorkee, and Haridwar. With pit stops, plan for 6-6.5 hours total.


### Is the Delhi-Rishikesh road safe for self-drive?

Absolutely. The entire route is a well-maintained national highway with fuel stations, emergency services, and highway patrol. There are no mountain driving sections — the road is flat through to Haridwar, with a gentle riverside stretch to Rishikesh.


### Can I drive further from Rishikesh to Badrinath or Kedarnath?

Yes, but the roads beyond Rishikesh into the Garhwal Himalayas are challenging mountain roads. An SUV is essential, and only experienced mountain drivers should attempt these routes. Check BRO road status during monsoon season.


### What are the toll charges from Delhi to Rishikesh?

Total toll charges are approximately ₹350-450 one way. All Brothers rental cars include active FASTag for seamless toll crossing.


### Should I book a return trip or do a one-way drop?

If you plan to stay in Rishikesh for 2+ days and return to Delhi, keeping the rental car is most cost-effective. For one-way trips (flying out of Jolly Grant Airport), a one-way drop option is available at checkout.
