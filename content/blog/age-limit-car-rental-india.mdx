## Why is the Minimum Age 21 and Not 18?

While Indian driving licences can be issued at 18 years of age, most rental companies — including Brothers Car Rental — set the minimum at 21 for several important reasons:


- **Insurance requirements:** Insurance companies classify drivers under 21 as high-risk. Most comprehensive rental insurance policies require the primary driver to be 21+.
- **Driving experience:** A minimum of 1 year of active driving experience is required to safely operate a rental vehicle, especially on Indian highways and mountain roads.
- **Liability considerations:** Drivers under 21 statistically have higher accident rates. The age restriction protects both the renter and other road users.


## What if I'm Under 21?

If you're between 18-20 with a valid driving licence, your options are limited but not zero:


- **Add an older co-driver:** If a travel companion aged 21+ holds a valid licence, they can be listed as the primary renter. You can be added as an additional authorized driver on some plans.
- **Contact us directly:** In rare cases, we may accommodate 18-20 year olds for hatchback-only rentals with enhanced security deposits and additional verification. [Reach out to our team](/browse "Contact for young driver rental").


## Maximum Age Limit

Brothers Car Rental has no upper age limit as long as you hold a valid, non-expired driving licence and can provide valid KYC documents. Senior drivers are welcome to rent any vehicle in our fleet.


## Licence Validity Rules

- **Expired licences:** Not accepted under any circumstances. Your licence must be current and valid on the date of vehicle pickup.
- **Learner's licence:** Not accepted. Only full (permanent) driving licences are valid for car rental.
- **International tourists:** Must hold an International Driving Permit (IDP) paired with their home country licence. [See our document checklist](/blog/documents-needed-to-rent-a-car "Required documents for car rental").


## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a 19-year-old rent a car in India?

Most rental companies require a minimum age of 21. At Brothers Car Rental, the standard minimum is 21 with at least 1 year of driving experience. Contact us for special arrangements if you're 18-20.


### Is there an age limit for renting an SUV?

Standard SUVs require a minimum age of 21. Premium and luxury SUVs may require 25+ due to higher insurance requirements and the vehicle's power output.


### Can I rent a car with a learner's permit?

No. A full, permanent driving licence is mandatory for all self-drive car rentals in India. Learner's permits are not accepted.


### Does the age limit apply to additional drivers?

Yes. All authorized drivers listed on the rental agreement must meet the minimum age and licence requirements for the vehicle category being rented.


### What documents prove my age?

Your driving licence contains your date of birth. Additionally, your Aadhaar card, passport, or voter ID serves as age verification. No separate age-proof document is needed beyond your standard KYC.
