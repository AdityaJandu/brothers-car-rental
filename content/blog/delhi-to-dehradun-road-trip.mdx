## Route Details

### Delhi → Meerut (70 km, ~1 hour)

The Delhi-Meerut Expressway is India's widest at 14 lanes. This opening stretch gets you out of NCR traffic in record time. Maintain 100 km/h and enjoy the smooth ride.


### Meerut → Roorkee (115 km, ~2 hours)

Continue on NH58/NH334 through Muzaffarnagar. The road is wide, flat, and passes through sugarcane and wheat farmland. Multiple fuel stations and dhabas line the route.


### Roorkee → Dehradun via Haridwar Bypass (70 km, ~2 hours)

Two sub-route options here:


- **Via Haridwar Bypass (recommended):** A newer bypass road avoids Haridwar city traffic. Faster and more comfortable, especially during Kanwar Yatra season.
- **Via Haridwar City:** If you want to stop at Haridwar for a Ganga dip or darshan. Adds 30-45 minutes depending on traffic.

## Top Pit Stops

- **Cheetal Grand, Modipuram (near Meerut):** One of the best highway restaurants in North India. Clean facilities, fast food, and convenience shopping.
- **Muzaffarnagar Dhabas:** Authentic highway dhabas serving parathas, dal, and fresh lassi.
- **Roorkee:** Home to IIT Roorkee. Good midpoint for fuel and a quick stretch break.
- **Dehradun Entry — Rispana Bridge Area:** The first glimpse of the Doon Valley mountains. A nice photo moment before entering the city.


## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Delhi-Dehradun route the same as Delhi-Rishikesh?

Almost. Both routes follow the same highway until the Haridwar area, where you either continue north to Dehradun or branch east to Rishikesh. The route to Dehradun via Haridwar bypass is the most efficient.


### Can I drive to Dehradun at night?

The highway portion is safe for night driving with well-lit toll plazas. Avoid the rural stretches near Muzaffarnagar after midnight when truck traffic peaks and visibility drops. Daytime driving is always recommended for the best experience.


### What is the best day and time to drive?

Start at 5-6 AM on weekdays for the emptiest roads. Weekend mornings see heavier tourist traffic from Delhi toward Uttarakhand. Avoid Friday evenings entirely — the NCR-to-hills weekend exodus creates massive jams.

### Is there a faster route via Eastern Peripheral Expressway?

The Delhi-Meerut Expressway is currently the fastest route. The Eastern Peripheral Expressway is useful for traffic originating from South Delhi or Gurgaon — it lets you bypass Delhi entirely and join NH58 near Meerut. Check Google Maps live traffic for the best option on your departure day.
