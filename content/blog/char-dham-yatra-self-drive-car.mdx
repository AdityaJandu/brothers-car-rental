## Recommended Itinerary: 12 Days

1. **Day 1:** Delhi/Dehradun → Haridwar (collect blessings at Har Ki Pauri). Night stay: Haridwar.
2. **Day 2:** Haridwar → Barkot (210 km, 7-8 hrs). Scenic drive via Mussoorie and Purola. Night stay: Barkot.
3. **Day 3:** Barkot → Yamunotri (trek 6 km from Janki Chatti). Return and stay: Barkot.
4. **Day 4:** Barkot → Uttarkashi (100 km, 4 hrs). Via Dharasu bend — one of the most scenic drives in Uttarakhand. Night stay: Uttarkashi.
5. **Day 5:** Uttarkashi → Gangotri (100 km, 4 hrs). Darshan at Gangotri Temple. Return and stay: Uttarkashi.
6. **Day 6:** Uttarkashi → Guptkashi (220 km, 8-9 hrs). Long but dramatic drive via Tehri Dam and Rudraprayag. Night stay: Guptkashi.
7. **Day 7:** Guptkashi → Kedarnath (trek 18 km from Gaurikund, or helicopter). Night stay: Kedarnath.
8. **Day 8:** Kedarnath → Guptkashi (return trek). Rest. Night stay: Guptkashi.
9. **Day 9:** Guptkashi → Joshimath (180 km, 6-7 hrs). Via Chamoli and Pipalkoti. Night stay: Joshimath.
10. **Day 10:** Joshimath → Badrinath (45 km, 1.5 hrs). Darshan at Badrinath Temple. Night stay: Badrinath.
11. **Day 11:** Badrinath → Rudraprayag (160 km, 5-6 hrs). Relaxed drive back. Night stay: Rudraprayag.
12. **Day 12:** Rudraprayag → Rishikesh/Haridwar (165 km, 5 hrs). Return car. End of yatra.


## Why Self-Drive for Char Dham?

- **Devotional pace:** Stop at every prayag (confluence), every temple, every viewpoint. No bus schedule rushes you.
- **Family comfort:** Elderly family members travel in air-conditioned, padded comfort instead of hard bus seats.
- **Luggage freedom:** Carry puja materials, warm clothing, medicines, and personal items without weight restrictions.
- **Route flexibility:** Take detours to Auli, Chopta, or Tehri Dam if time permits.


## Vehicle Requirement: SUV Only

A sedan or hatchback is absolutely not suitable for the Char Dham circuit. The route involves:


- Steep mountain gradients requiring strong engine torque
- Narrow roads with blind curves and sheer drop-offs
- Unpaved stretches, especially near Yamunotri and Kedarnath approach
- Possible landslide debris requiring ground clearance

## Essential Tips for Char Dham Self-Drive

- **Start each day before 6 AM:** Mountain driving should only happen during daylight. Early starts ensure you reach each night halt before sunset.
- **Carry cash:** ATMs and UPI are unreliable in remote Uttarakhand. Carry ₹15,000-20,000 in cash for the entire circuit.
- **Fuel strategy:** Fill up at Rishikesh, Uttarkashi, Rudraprayag, Chamoli. These are the reliable fuel stations on the circuit.
- **Medical preparedness:** Carry altitude sickness medication, basic first aid, and personal prescriptions. Medical facilities are scarce beyond Rudraprayag.
- **Registration:** Register at the Char Dham biometric registration centres (mandatory) at Rishikesh or Haridwar before starting.

### What is the best month for Char Dham by car?

May-June (pre-monsoon) and September-October (post-monsoon) offer the best driving conditions. Avoid July-August when landslides frequently close roads.


### Is the entire circuit drivable, or are treks involved?

Yamunotri requires a 6 km trek (or pony ride) from Janki Chatti. Kedarnath requires an 18 km trek from Gaurikund (or helicopter). Badrinath and Gangotri are directly accessible by car.
