Delhi's strategic position at the crossroads of North India makes it the ultimate road-trip launchpad. Within a 5-8 hour drive, you can reach hill stations, desert forts, holy cities, and UNESCO World Heritage sites. Here are the **10 best road trips from Delhi** ranked by driving experience, destination appeal, and the ideal rental car for each route.


## 1. Delhi to Jaipur — The Royal Highway (280 km, ~5 hours)

The NH48 expressway to the Pink City is India's smoothest highway drive. Visit Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, and the vibrant bazaars of Rajasthan's capital. **Best car:** [Premium sedan](/browse?category=sedan "Sedan rental Delhi to Jaipur") for flat highway cruising. [Read our complete Delhi-Jaipur guide](/blog/delhi-to-jaipur-road-trip "Delhi to Jaipur driving guide").


## 2. Delhi to Agra — Taj Mahal Express (230 km, ~3.5 hours)

The Yamuna Expressway delivers you to the Taj Mahal in record time. The perfect day trip — leave after breakfast, return for dinner. **Best car:** Any category works on this world-class expressway. [Read our Agra day-trip guide](/blog/delhi-to-agra-road-trip "Delhi to Agra road trip").


## 3. Delhi to Rishikesh — Adventure Capital (240 km, ~5.5 hours)

White-water rafting, yoga retreats, and the iconic Ganga Aarti. The drive is flat highway all the way to Haridwar, with a scenic riverside stretch to Rishikesh. **Best car:** Sedan for the highway; [SUV if continuing to the mountains](/browse?category=suv "SUV for Rishikesh adventure"). [Read our Rishikesh guide](/blog/delhi-to-rishikesh-road-trip "Delhi to Rishikesh by car").


## 4. Delhi to Mussoorie — Queen of Hills (290 km, ~6.5 hours)

Escape the capital's heat for misty mountain air. The drive includes both flat highways and a dramatic 35 km mountain ascent. **Best car:** [Compact SUV](/browse?category=suv "SUV for Mussoorie") for the hill climb. [Read our Mussoorie guide](/blog/delhi-to-mussoorie-road-trip-car-rental "Delhi to Mussoorie road trip").


## 5. Delhi to Shimla — Colonial Hill Station (350 km, ~7 hours)

The former British summer capital perched in the pines. Drive through Chandigarh and ascend via Solan. **Best car:** SUV or premium sedan. [Read our Shimla guide](/blog/delhi-to-shimla-road-trip "Delhi to Shimla by car").


## 6. Delhi to Haridwar — Sacred Pilgrimage (215 km, ~5 hours)

One of Hinduism's holiest cities, famous for the Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri. All-highway route. **Best car:** [Family sedan](/browse?category=sedan "Sedan for Haridwar pilgrimage"). [Read our Haridwar guide](/blog/delhi-to-haridwar-road-trip "Delhi to Haridwar by car").


## 7. Delhi to Jim Corbett — Wildlife Safari (260 km, ~6 hours)

India's oldest national park and a tiger reserve. The highway drive is easy; the last 30 km into the park requires an SUV. **Best car:** [SUV (mandatory for park entry)](/browse?category=suv "SUV for Corbett Safari").


## 8. Delhi to Chandigarh — City Beautiful (260 km, ~4.5 hours)

Le Corbusier's masterpiece of urban planning. The NH44 highway is wide and fast. Use Chandigarh as a base for Shimla, Manali, and Kasauli trips. **Best car:** Any highway-capable vehicle.


## 9. Delhi to Nainital — Lake District (300 km, ~7 hours)

The charming lake town of Kumaon. The drive combines flat highway with a scenic mountain ascent through Bhimtal and Naukuchiatal. **Best car:** Compact SUV for the mountain sections.


## 10. Delhi to Lansdowne — Hidden Gem (260 km, ~6 hours)

A little-known cantonment town in the Garhwal hills. Quiet, uncrowded, and surrounded by oak and pine forests. **Best car:** Any SUV for the last 30 km of hill road. [Read our Lansdowne guide](/blog/delhi-to-lansdowne-road-trip "Delhi to Lansdowne by car").

## General Self-Drive Road Trip Tips

- **Start Early:** Leave Delhi before 6 AM to beat the NCR traffic.
- **FASTag Ready:** All major highways require FASTag. Brothers vehicles come pre-equipped.
- **Offline Maps:** Download offline maps for destinations beyond the highway — network coverage drops in hills and forests.
- **Fuel Strategy:** Fill up in Delhi/NCR where fuel is cheapest. Hill station fuel prices carry a premium.
- **Documents:** Keep your driving licence, rental agreement, and photo ID in the car at all times. [See our document checklist](/blog/documents-needed-to-rent-a-car "Documents for car rental India").
