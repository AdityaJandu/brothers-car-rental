## What's Included vs What You Pay

- **Included:** Comprehensive insurance, scheduled servicing, tyre replacement (wear-based), battery replacement, roadside assistance, FASTag.
- **You pay:** Fuel, toll charges, parking, traffic violation fines, excessive damage beyond wear and tear.

## Who Should Choose Long-Term Rental?

- **Corporate transferees:** Posted to Delhi for 6 months? Subscribe, drive, return when you transfer.
- **Startup founders:** Preserve capital for your business. A monthly car subscription is an operating expense, not a capital expense.
- **Families needing a second car:** Your spouse starts a new job and needs a commuter car. Subscribe for the needed duration.
- **NRIs on extended visits:** 2-6 month India stays are perfectly served by monthly subscriptions.
- **Pre-purchase testers:** Live with a specific car model for a month before committing ₹15 lakh to buy it.


## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I take a long-term rental car on outstation trips?

Yes. All long-term rental cars carry all-India permits. Drive anywhere — city, highway, or mountain.


### What happens if I need the car for longer than originally planned?

Simply extend your subscription with a call. Extensions are seamless and charged at the same per-month rate. No re-booking or new paperwork required.


### Are long-term rental cars new or old?

Brothers maintains a fleet of vehicles typically 0-3 years old. All vehicles undergo comprehensive inspection and servicing between subscriptions.


### Can my spouse/family member also drive the long-term rental?

Yes. Additional authorized drivers can be added to the subscription at no extra cost, provided they meet our age and licence requirements.


### Is the deposit higher for long-term rentals?

Long-term rental deposits are comparable to short-term deposits (₹10,000-25,000 depending on vehicle). They're fully refundable at subscription end.
