Living in Delhi means enduring traffic, pollution, and crowds for five days a week. But it also means being within a 3-6 hour drive of some of India's most beautiful destinations. A **self-drive rental car** turns any weekend into an escape — no train tickets to book, no bus schedules to follow. Here are **10 weekend getaways near Delhi** ranked by driving distance.


## Under 3 Hours from Delhi

### 1. Neemrana Fort Palace (115 km, 2 hours)

A stunning 15th-century fort converted into a heritage hotel. Even day-trippers can enjoy the zip-line, rooftop restaurant, and panoramic Aravalli views. The NH48 highway makes this a smooth, fast drive in any [sedan](/browse?category=sedan "Sedan for Neemrana").


### 2. Agra — Taj Mahal (230 km, 3 hours)

The world's most famous monument is just 3 hours away on the Yamuna Expressway. See the Taj at sunrise and return by dinner. [Read our Agra day-trip guide](/blog/delhi-to-agra-road-trip "Delhi to Agra day trip").


### 3. Mathura-Vrindavan (170 km, 2.5 hours)

A spiritual weekend in the birthplace of Lord Krishna. Visit the Krishna Janmabhoomi, Banke Bihari Temple, and the colourful Vrindavan ashrams. Perfect for a budget [hatchback trip](/browse?category=hatchback "Budget car for Mathura").


## 3-5 Hours from Delhi

### 4. Jim Corbett National Park (260 km, 5 hours)

India's oldest national park and tiger reserve. Book a jeep safari, stay at a jungle lodge, and wake up to the sounds of the forest. [SUV mandatory](/browse?category=suv "SUV for Corbett") for park entry roads.


### 5. Jaipur — Pink City (280 km, 5 hours)

Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, bazaar shopping, and Rajasthani cuisine. The NH48 expressway makes Jaipur a comfortable 5-hour drive. [Read our Jaipur road-trip guide](/blog/delhi-to-jaipur-road-trip "Delhi to Jaipur guide").


### 6. Rishikesh — Adventure Capital (240 km, 5 hours)

Rafting, bungee jumping, yoga, and the iconic Ganga Aarti. The perfect weekend for adventure seekers. [Read our Rishikesh drive guide](/blog/delhi-to-rishikesh-road-trip "Delhi to Rishikesh guide").


### 7. Chandigarh — City Beautiful (260 km, 4.5 hours)

Le Corbusier's planned city offers Rock Garden, Sukhna Lake, and a gateway to Shimla and Kasauli. Wide, traffic-free roads make driving here a pleasure.


## 5-6 Hours from Delhi

### 8. Mussoorie — Queen of Hills (290 km, 6 hours)

The classic Delhi hill-station escape. Cool mountain air, Mall Road strolls, and stunning Doon Valley views. [Read our Mussoorie road-trip guide](/blog/delhi-to-mussoorie-road-trip-car-rental "Delhi to Mussoorie guide").


### 9. Lansdowne — Silent Hills (260 km, 6 hours)

The hill station the crowds haven't discovered. Car-free cantonment, oak forests, and total peace. [Read our Lansdowne guide](/blog/delhi-to-lansdowne-road-trip "Delhi to Lansdowne guide").


### 10. Nainital — Lake District (300 km, 6 hours)

Naini Lake, colonial charm, and the Kumaon hills. Combine with Bhimtal and Naukuchiatal for a multi-lake weekend.

## Weekend Road Trip Tips

- **Leave Friday evening or Saturday dawn:** Beat the weekend exodus traffic by starting before 6 AM Saturday or after 8 PM Friday.
- **Book accommodation in advance:** Weekend stays at popular destinations sell out fast, especially during October-March peak season.
- **Return by Sunday 4 PM:** The Delhi-bound traffic on Sunday evenings (5-9 PM) is brutal. Start your return drive early.
